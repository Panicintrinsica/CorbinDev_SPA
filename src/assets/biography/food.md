I would not call myself a foodie but I am an omnivore with an above-average appreciation for quality that is decidedly "*Unamerican*".  I enjoy cooking and baking and I quite good at both, with an extremely strong bias toward European cuisine, particularly Scandinavian, French, and Greek. I love Italian as well, but my fondness there tends to be for *actual* Italian food and not the Italian-American horrors spawned by chain restaurants.

I am also a big fan of Chinese, Japanese, Korean, and Thai, but again, the good actual things and not the takeout versions drowning in sugar.

You could say I am snobbish in this regard, and I wont not contest this point. I've arrived at a point where I would rather not eat at all then subject myself to bad food, and will do almost anything to avoid eating at fast food establishments unless I am being socially forced into it. I don't want ultra-processed breads laced with sugar, and there is a good chance I would rather starve then buy anything that comes cooked out of a box.

My favourite baked good is probably *Lemon Coffee Cake*, and my favourite entrée is likely *Köttbullar*, although I also adore a *Steak & Cilantro Salad* when I can get everything to do it perfectly. When I am tired and I just need to make food in a hurry, my go-to is usually *Norwegian Cod with Dill, Lemon, and Caraway*. If I had to claim a "signature dish" it would likely be what I've been calling "*Forbidden Sirloins*" because I was once told I should not use Juniper with beef, but I am going to ***keep doing it anyway***.

My *Spaghetti all'Assassina* is also pretty great with a brutally tannic red.
