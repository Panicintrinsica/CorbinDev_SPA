These days my musical tastes are pretty centered on Progressive Trance & House, namely coming from the artist catalogues of _Anjuna_, _This Never Happened_, and sometimes Armada.

I went to multiple ABGT weekenders thereafter, and would absolutely love to find the time to go to more shows. Sadly for me, they're basically never conveniently located and required international trips more often than not.
