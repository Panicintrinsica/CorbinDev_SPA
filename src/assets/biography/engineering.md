Software Engineering was never really my original goal, and was more of something that happened to me over time.  The first time I remember ever touching any code was when I picked to LUA to modify the game [_Drakan_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drakan:_Order_of_the_Flame) somewhere around 2002. Shortly after that came Unreal Script and C++ for [_Unreal Tournament 2004_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unreal_Tournament_2004). At that point in time, my interest was principally rooted in game development itself. All I knew was that I wanted to make games, and that UnrealEd was a great entry point at the time thanks to the VTM's by 3DBuzz that were included in the UT2k4 special edition. From there I started to experiment in Game Design, 3D Modeling, Animation, Video Production, and Audio Engineering, and spent my entire teens trying to make the game dev thing happen with my friends.

While this was all happening, I was also playing [_EverQuest 2_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EverQuest_II) and placing far too much hope in _Vanguard: Saga of Heroes_ being the savior of MMOs. This, and the desire to have a game company, both led to me making a *lot* of websites for guilds and for myself, and as a consequence I started working with the classic quartet of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP.

By the time I hit my 20s, I had accidentally turned myself into a Web Developer and found myself spawning and managing website for multiple employers on both hourly and contract gigs.

For a handful of reasons, including losing a job opportunity because a client thought I was underqualified, I decided to go back to university and get an actual degree in the subject.
I graduated from [Missouri State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Missouri_State_University) with a BS in *Information Technology & Application Development* in December 2024 and then began working at MSU as an **Application Developer** in March 2025.

Outside my day job, I am currently working on ERIS as my primary personal project, which combines both my historic interest in game development with my current love of writing hyper performant software. I am also working on something I am currently calling Guildhall, which I aim to be a self-hostable community platform in the vein of old PHP CMS packages I grew up modding, only, you know, not using PHP and with 20 years experience in CSS and JS
