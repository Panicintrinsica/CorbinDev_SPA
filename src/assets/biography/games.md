Games are my primary form of entertainment, though I do not identify as a "gamer" any more than someone would identify as a "TV Watcher". Consuming a category of media is not a personality type or an identity.

I don't feel like I have a single "favorite" game. I love a lot of games for different reasons, but gun to my head, I would probably pick [Resident Evil (1996)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Evil_(1996_video_game)). While I have a lot of fond memories from the PSX and N64 games, nothing has quite stuck the way RE did.

Eventually, on a different site, I intend to have a more extensive list of games I've played and my various thoughts on them, but I'll say other important highlights for me are [Tomb Raider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomb_Raider_(1996_video_game)), [Myst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myst), [Curse of Monkey Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Curse_of_Monkey_Island), The [Ultima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultima_(series)) franchise, [Unreal Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unreal_Tournament), [Dungeon Keeper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Keeper), [Diablo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diablo_(series)), [Metal Gear Solid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_Gear_Solid_(1998_video_game)), [Age of Empires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Empires), [King's Field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King%27s_Field), and [Final Fantasy VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_VII).

For sixth generation games we have [SSX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SSX_(2000_video_game)), [Dark Cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_Cloud), [Devil May Cry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devil_May_Cry), [Dungeon Siege](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dungeon_Siege).

[EverQuest II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EverQuest_II) is by far the most "important" game in my life, since it was directly responsible for my two more significant friendships. It's in no way hyperbolic to claim playing EQ2 is _directly_ responsible for my personality and current mental health. The timeline in which I never picked up EQ2, and never met Ren & Anna, is a far, far, far worse one.

I do enjoy the survival/building genre quite a bit. No Man's Sky, Valheim, Nightingale, even the original Ark. That I actually play them with anyone else so I always feel like I am getting a lesser experience out of them. Oh well.

Most recently the games I have been rotating through are [Elite Dangerous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elite_Dangerous), [Path of Exile 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Path_of_Exile_2), and [Final Fantasy XIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Final_Fantasy_XIV). I am also slowly working my way though the entire Final Fantasy series in numeric order at the moment, and am back up to VII at time of writing.
