I have a reasonably broad taste in literature, although I tend to read more fiction than anything else.

Joe Abercrombie's  [*First Law*](https://www.goodreads.com/series/43644-the-first-law),  Scott Lynch's *[Gentleman Bastard](https://www.goodreads.com/series/43531-gentleman-bastard)*, and Patrick Rothfess' [Kingkiller Chronicles](https://www.goodreads.com/series/45262-the-kingkiller-chronicle) are easy and non-controversial favorites. I've read a plurality of Brandon Sanderson's work, and have obviously given my time to Tolkien and Martin. 

I have a soft spot for Elizabeth Moon's *[The Deed of Paksenarrion](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/96281.The_Deed_of_Paksenarrion)* , which is one of the few books I have read more than twice. It is very much the "comfort book" from my teens.  [*The Iron Dragon's Daughter*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/25781.The_Iron_Dragon_s_Daughter) by Michael Swanwick is the weirdest book I have ever read. And I would say that Max Gladstone's *[Craft Sequence](https://www.goodreads.com/author/show/3405346.Max_Gladstone)* has one of the more interesting worlds I've run across.

[*The Diamond Age*](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/827.The_Diamond_Age) by Neal Stephenson was recommended to me by my BFF, as it was her favourite book as a teen, and is an interesting bit of post-cyberpunk that is still worth reading. I've been meaning to get around to the Expanse series, but it's not happened yet. 

In terms of non-fiction, I tend to enjoy mostly history and anthropology. I can't give less of a shit about the biographies of politicians and actors, but I'll happily read books about computing history like *[Open](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/17290907-open)* by Rod Canion or *[The Idea Factory](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/11797471-the-idea-factory)* by Jon Gertner.

As a classicist at heart, I also love leafing through dense history textbooks on everything from medieval economies, Roman legal texts, Greek plays, or most direct analyses like *[The Darkening Age](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/35450727-the-darkening-age)* by Catherine Nixey
